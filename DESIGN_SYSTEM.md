# RACO website design system

Last updated: 2026-08-15

## Direction

Bold, modern and editorial with industrial precision. Pages should feel composed rather than templated: large type, decisive colour, asymmetric grids, purposeful imagery and restrained motion.

## Core palette

- Charcoal: `#404040`
- Ink: `#07110d`
- Cream: `#f5f5f0`
- White: `#ffffff`
- Lime: `#c7f23a`
- Orange: `#ff6a1a`
- Magenta: `#ff3db8`
- Purple: `#7a3cff`
- Cyan: `#00e0ff`

Dark sections use charcoal or ink without blue tint. Orange is the primary highlighted text colour in dark mode.

## Page identities

- Business Systems Launch: cream, ink and orange; structured workflow lines and an editorial systems-diagram character.
- AI Automation Pilot: charcoal, cream, orange and magenta; a controlled signal-flow character rather than generic AI gradients.

## Interaction

- Motion should explain hierarchy or progression.
- Hover fills travel left to right where appropriate.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Controls must remain keyboard accessible and retain visible focus states.

## Content rules

- Lead with business outcomes, not technical terminology.
- Explain specialist terms when they are necessary.
- One primary action per section.
- Never imply that illustrative demonstrations are client results.
