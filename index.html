Frequently asked

Important questions,
answered directly.

Do you deliver conventional software as well as AI systems?+

Yes. We deliver complete web, mobile, SaaS, cloud and enterprise software projects. AI is a core capability, not a requirement for every engagement.

Can RACO work with our existing engineering team?+

Yes. We can own a complete project, form a dedicated pod or add specialised software and AI capability to your existing team.

Will we retain our data and intellectual property?+

Engagement terms are structured so clients retain ownership of their project deliverables and business data. Deployment architecture is designed around your privacy and sovereignty requirements.

Are discovery sessions and systems audits free?+

Yes. The first discovery conversation and a focused review of one current system or workflow are free. Implementation, production rollout, expansion and ongoing support are paid engagements.

What happens after launch?+

We provide monitoring, optimisation, security review, model improvement and continued feature development through our managed services model.
