# RACO website project context

Last updated: 2026-08-15

## Purpose

The website must generate qualified conversations for two productised services rather than present a large, undifferentiated capability catalogue.

## Primary offers

1. **The Business Systems Launch** — design and build one important operational system around a clearly defined business workflow.
2. **The AI Automation Pilot** — automate one repetitive, high-value workflow with controlled AI and a clear path to production.

## Free entry points

- **Free discovery session** — a focused first conversation to understand the problem and determine whether RACO is a suitable partner.
- **Free systems audit** — a bounded review of one current system or workflow, resulting in initial observations and a recommended next step. It is not a security, compliance, architecture or organisation-wide audit.

Implementation, production rollout, expansion and managed support are paid services. Public pages do not publish project prices during Stage 1.

## Primary markets

1. RMG and manufacturing
2. Education
3. BPO and customer operations
4. Growth-stage digital companies
5. Financial services, subject to accurate security and data-processing qualification

## Operating model

- One UK contracting entity serves clients globally.
- Bristol supports commercial leadership and international client relationships.
- Dhaka is a registered operation supporting engineering delivery, regional relationships and the AI Lab.
- Relationship management and support can be assigned by country and timezone.
- Data may be hosted or processed in the UK and Bangladesh. Hosting, processing locations and delivery-team access are agreed per engagement.
- Legal entity names are not used in ordinary marketing copy.

## Proof policy

Until approved client case studies are available, demonstrations use the name **XYZ Company** and the label **Demo company — illustrative engagement**.

Demo content must not contain fabricated metrics, numerical targets, testimonials, logos or claims of completed client results. It explains the situation, proposed system and intended business value only.

## Commercial journeys

Business software: free discovery or systems audit → Business Systems Launch → additional modules → managed product support.

AI automation: free discovery or systems audit → AI Automation Pilot → AI Production Rollout → managed AI operations.
