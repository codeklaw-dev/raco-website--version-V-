Overview

From isolated checks
to one quality workflow.

This page demonstrates the RACO case-study architecture without presenting an unapproved client engagement as fact. The delivery pattern begins where visual inspection is manual, inconsistent or disconnected from production data.

The target system connects image capture, model inference, operator review, exception handling and evidence retention. Decisions remain visible, and people retain control at uncertain or consequential points.

The solution pattern

Vision is one layer.
The workflow is the product.

A useful inspection system must account for changing conditions, uncertain predictions, operator experience and the downstream consequence of a defect decision.

  • Controlled image capture and data labelling
  • Model evaluation against operational acceptance criteria
  • Human review for uncertainty and high-impact decisions
  • Traceable results connected to production context
  • Monitoring for model quality and environmental change

Intended results

Better evidence.
Faster response.

Production metrics would be added only after client approval and verification. These are the qualitative outcomes the architecture is designed to enable.
