FAQ

Choosing the
right model.

What if our scope is still unclear?+

Begin with discovery. It creates enough evidence to choose a project, dedicated-team or staged programme shape.

Can the model change later?+

Yes. A project can transition into managed support or a dedicated team as priorities become clearer.

How quickly can a team start?+

Timing depends on the specialist mix and onboarding requirements. Discovery is usually the fastest practical starting point.

Can we start with one specialist?+

Yes, where the work can be safely integrated into your existing leadership and delivery process.
