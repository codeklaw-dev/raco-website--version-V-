What a case study should prove

Clear before.
Clear after.

A useful case study should help a buyer understand the operating problem, what was built and how success was judged.

01 / Problem

The operating constraint

Who is affected, where the current workflow breaks and why the problem matters now.

02 / System

The delivery shape

The product, automation or AI capability—and the boundaries that keep the scope useful.

03 / Evidence

The proof plan

The baseline, adoption signals and business measures agreed before the work is called successful.

Publication standard

When client work publishes,
the evidence comes with it.

  • Client approval and truthful attribution
  • Named operating context and delivery scope
  • Documented baseline and measurement method
  • No invented metrics, testimonials or outcomes
